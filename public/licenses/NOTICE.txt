amphi — licences of the published site
======================================

amphi's own source code is under the MIT licence:
https://github.com/maxgfr/amphi/blob/main/LICENSE

The site as published (https://maxgfr.github.io/amphi/) also ships eSpeak NG,
which the voices of this device use to turn text into phonemes. eSpeak NG is
under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later, so the site taken
as a whole is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Its text is in GPL-3.0.txt,
next to this file.

eSpeak NG in this site
----------------------

  vendor/piper_phonemize.wasm   eSpeak NG and piper-phonemize, compiled to
                                WebAssembly by @diffusionstudio/piper-wasm 1.0.0
  vendor/espeak-ng.data         eSpeak NG's data, kept to the English, French
                                and German dictionaries by scripts/espeak.ts
  voice.js                      the Emscripten loader of that build, with its
                                file list rewritten by scripts/espeak.ts

Corresponding source
--------------------

  amphi, its build and the trimming of eSpeak NG's data:
    https://github.com/maxgfr/amphi
  The WebAssembly build, with the exact steps that produced it:
    https://www.npmjs.com/package/@diffusionstudio/piper-wasm/v/1.0.0
  eSpeak NG (the fork it was built from):
    https://github.com/rhasspy/espeak-ng
  piper-phonemize:
    https://github.com/wide-video/piper-phonemize

Other parts
-----------

The rest of the bundled code keeps its own licence, all compatible with the
GPL: MIT (React, onnxruntime-web, sql.js, pptxgenjs, marked, minisearch,
fflate, @noble/hashes, zod, llm-connect, llm-models), ISC (idb), Apache-2.0
(the AI SDK, Transformers.js, pdf.js), MPL-2.0 (mediabunny) and
MPL-2.0 or Apache-2.0 (DOMPurify). The Archivo font is under the SIL Open
Font License (fonts/OFL.txt).

Speech models are downloaded from Hugging Face when first used, not shipped
with the site: Kokoro (Apache-2.0), Piper voices for French (Jessica, Pierre:
CC-BY-SA 4.0; Siwis: CC-BY 4.0) and German (Kerstin, Thorsten: CC0), and the
transcription models, each under the licence shown on its model page.
